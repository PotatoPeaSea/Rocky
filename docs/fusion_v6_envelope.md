# Rocky v6: mechanical envelope for the integrated control PCB

Source: Fusion document **"Pentapod Walker v6"**, generated by `fusion/pentapod_build_v6.py`.
v6 is v5 with every modelled wire and all module electronics removed; the plates, deck, dome and legs are unchanged.

**Frame.** The model frame is in mm. The origin is the body centre on the hip-servo axis plane, +z is up and the angles are measured CCW from +x.
Leg *n* (1..5) lies on the axis at 90°, 162°, 234°, 306° and 18°. Leg 1 is on +y, and that is the only "front" defined in the model.
The dome posts / tie rods sit at the gap angles 126°, 198°, 270°, 342° and 54°.

### Height available above the deck top (z 55.5) before the dome inner surface
The dome inner surface is a sphere with R_i = 156.89 centred at (0, 0, -45.29), so z_inner(r) = -45.29 + sqrt(156.89² - r²).

| r (mm) | 0 | 20 | 30 | 40 | 50 | 60 | 65 | 70 | 75 | 78 | 82 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| dome inner z | 111.6 | 110.3 | 108.7 | 106.4 | 103.4 | 99.7 | 97.5 | 95.1 | 92.5 | 90.8 | 88.5 |
| clearance above deck top | 56.1 | 54.8 | 53.2 | 50.9 | 47.9 | 44.2 | 42.0 | 39.6 | 37.0 | 35.3 | 33.0 |

For a board sitting at z 60.5 (1.6 mm thick, top at 62.1), take off another 6.6 mm. That leaves **≥ 28 mm for top-side parts anywhere inside r 78**. Keep a 3 mm air gap to the dome, so plan on **≤ 25 mm component height at the rim** and ≤ 35 mm within r 60.

## 2. Logic deck

- **Outline:** a disc of **r 82** plus 5 round lobes of r 11 centred at r 88 on the gap angles. The lobes carry the dome posts, and the overall extent is r 99. The thickness is 3.5 mm and the deck spans z 52.0..55.5.
- **Tie-rod holes:** Ø 3.4 through the deck at the post centres, r 88. These take an M3 rod, which clamps the upper post, deck, lower post, top plate, standoff and bottom plate together.

| Post | angle | x | y |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | 126° | -51.7 | 71.2 |
| P2 | 198° | -83.7 | -27.2 |
| P3 | 270° | 0.0 | -88.0 |
| P4 | 342° | 83.7 | -27.2 |
| P5 | 54° | 51.7 | 71.2 |

- **Upper dome posts:** Ø 12 (r 6) on the deck at those points, z 55.5..78. Their inner edge is at r 82, so **keep the PCB inside r ≤ 79**.
- **Lower dome posts:** Ø 12 between the top plate and the deck (z 19.65..52) at the same points.

## 3. Raspberry Pi 5 + Active Cooler (kept, unchanged)

- **Board:** 85 × 56. The corners are at (-50.5, -28.0) and (34.5, 28.0), with the long axis along x. The underside is at z 60.5 and the top at 61.8.
- **Orientation:** the 40-pin GPIO runs along the **+y edge**, with pin 1 at the -x end. USB-A and RJ45 face **+x**. USB-C power, the 2 × micro-HDMI and the camera/display FPC connectors are on the **-y edge**. The PCIe FPC and the power button are at the **-x end**.
- **Mounting holes:** M2.5 at (-47.0, -24.5), (11.0, -24.5), (-47.0, 24.5) and (11.0, 24.5). They sit on 5 mm deck bosses (z 55.5 → 60.5).
- **GPIO header:** the plastic body spans x -43.4..7.4, y 21.9..27.1, and the pin tops are at z 70.4.
  - Pin 1 is at (-42.15, 23.25). The odd pins are at y 23.25 and the even pins at y 25.75. The pitch is 2.54 along +x.
  - Pins 2 and 4 are 5 V and pin 6 is GND. Pin 8 is GPIO14/TXD at (-34.53, 25.75) and pin 10 is GPIO15/RXD at (-31.99, 25.75).
  - The through-hole tails reach down to z 59.9.
- **USB-C power:** the receptacle spans x -43.8..-34.8 (centre x ≈ -39.3), y -29.2..-21.8, z 60.8..65.1. It opens toward -y.
  - The v5 right-angle pigtail occupied x -60.7..-33.1, y -46.4..-23.0, z 59.6..67.3. That pigtail has been removed.
- **USB-A / RJ45 stack:** x 15.9..37.6, y -25.7..26.3, up to z 78.0.
- **Underside:** the microSD slot spans x -48.3..-36.8, y -5.9..6.1 and reaches down to z 59.1.
- **Active Cooler:** x -50.6..14.2, y -22.0..21.5, z 61.1..75.3. Its fan pulls air from above, so don't cover it.
- **Pi 5 + cooler keep-out box (including connector overhangs):** **x -51.0..37.6, y -29.2..28.0, z 59.0..78.0**.
  - Add 2 mm of margin, giving x -53..40, y -31..30.
  - If the Pi is still powered from its USB-C port, also keep **x -61..-33, y -47..-29, z 59..68** free for a right-angle plug.

## 4. Free deck area around the Pi (inside r 79, outside the Pi box x -53..40, y -31..30)

- **North band:** y 30..79, about 145 mm wide at y 30 and 104 mm wide at y 60. This is the largest area; it held the v5 ESP32 perfboard and IMU.
- **South band:** y -31..-79, which mirrors the north band. It held the v5 BusLinker.
- **West strip:** x -79..-53, |y| up to ~55. It is only 26 mm wide and held the v5 Pololu.
- **East strip:** x 40..79, |y| up to ~68, 39 mm wide.
- **Total:** about 19,600 mm² of r-79 disc minus 5,700 mm² of Pi box ≈ **13,900 mm²** of usable board area in a single ring.
- **Bottom-side parts:** a board resting on the 5 mm bosses has 5 mm under it (deck top 55.5 → board underside 60.5), so keep bottom-side parts **≤ 4 mm**.

## 5. Legs: hip-servo connectors and cable pass-throughs

Each leg's hip servo lies radially between the plates, with its case spanning r 64.7..109.8. The **connector end faces the centre at r ≈ 64.7, z ≈ -1** (between the plates).
The leg-local origin of the v5 cable was (63.75, +4, -4), where local +x points radially out and local +y is CCW-tangential.

| Leg | axis | hip connector (x, y) @ r 64.7 | top-plate slot centre (r 57.25) | bottom-plate drop slot (r 75, axis-20°) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 90° | (0.0, 64.7) | (0.0, 57.2) | (25.7, 70.5) |
| 2 | 162° | (-61.5, 20.0) | (-54.4, 17.7) | (-59.1, 46.2) |
| 3 | 234° | (-38.0, -52.3) | (-33.7, -46.3) | (-62.2, -41.9) |
| 4 | 306° | (38.0, -52.3) | (33.7, -46.3) | (20.7, -72.1) |
| 5 | 18° | (61.5, 20.0) | (54.4, 17.7) | (75.0, -2.6) |

- **Top-plate branch slot (per leg):** 7.5 mm radial (r 53.5..61.0) × 10 mm tangential, through the top plate (z 15.65..19.65), on the leg axis directly above the hip connector. This is the way **up** from the servo bus to the electronics.
- **Bottom-plate drop slot:** 10 × 7. It carries the hip→femur stock cable under the coxa. Leg-internal cables run hip → femur → knee, and they also pass a 7 × 7 slot in the femur's -y arm and zip-tie anchors on the coxa. **These are real stock cables and stay; v6 just doesn't draw them.**
- **Deck to top plate:** the deck has **no** slots on the leg axes. At each leg angle there is open space outside the deck edge, from r 82 out to the dome (r ~110–120 at deck level). A cable can therefore drop over the deck edge, go down to the top plate (z 19.65), run inward to the branch slot at r 57 and go down to the connector.
  - Rough route from a board-edge connector at r ~74: 12 + 40 + 27 + 26 + 8 ≈ **115 mm**. A stock 20 cm HX servo cable reaches with slack.
  - The v5 holes A, B and C are not on the leg axes.
  - If a straighter path is wanted later, the deck could get a ~8 × 12 notch on each leg axis at r 74..82. That is not done yet and the plate geometry is unchanged.

## 6. Battery, loop key and the battery-lead path

- **3S 2200 mAh LiPo:** x -54.5..54.5, y -17..17, z -32.7..-8.6. It sits in the tray under the bottom plate and pokes up through the bottom plate's 113 × 43 bay.
  - Its XT60H-M lead lies on top of the pack: x -48..48, y -8.3..8.3, z -8.4..1.0.
  - The tray holds the pack with M3 heat-set inserts at (±61, 0) and (0, ±27).
- **XT60 loop key:** the socket is in the bottom plate at **(0, -68)**, through a 16.6 × 8.6 cut-out, spanning z -26.7..-5.5. The plug hangs below, z -46.8..-26.6, and is pulled from underneath.
- **v5 inline ATM fuse holder:** removed. The fuse moves onto the PCB.
- **Battery-lead path up to the deck:**
  1. Battery + goes to the loop-key socket (0, -68). Battery − runs directly.
  2. Both go up through the **top-plate power slot, 12 × 8 at (0, -56)**, which is kept.
  3. From there they run through the free space between the top plate and the deck (z 19.65..52).
  4. They then go up through **deck hole B, Ø 11 at (0, -71)**, onto the PCB.
- **Budget:** about 120 mm of 14 AWG per conductor from the loop key to the deck.
- **Board-side recommendation:** put the battery input (XT30 / XT60 / screw terminal) on the board's south edge near **(0, -70)**, directly over hole B. Leave a ~12 mm notch or hole in the board there, or keep the hole uncovered.

## 7. Recommended PCB outline and mounting

**Recommended: a ring ("donut") board coplanar with the Pi, sitting on the existing deck bosses.**

- **Outline:** a circle of **r 78 (Ø 156)** centred on (0, 0), with a rectangular **cut-out x -53..40, y -31..30** for the Pi 5 + cooler.
  - Leave a 1–2 mm radius in the cut-out corners.
  - If the Pi stays on USB-C power, extend the cut-out to x -61..-33, y -47..-29 around the plug. The better option is to power the Pi from the board through GPIO pins 2/4 (5 V) and 6 (GND), with `usb_max_current_enable=1` set in the Pi config.
- **Plane:** board underside at **z 60.5**, the same height as the Pi.
- **Mounting:** on the existing v5 bosses, all of whose tops are at z 60.5:
  - 4 × M2.5 at the BL positions **(±18, -63.5), (±18, -35.5)**.
  - 4 × M2 at the PB positions **(-30.8, 38.2), (-30.8, 63.8), (34.8, 38.2), (34.8, 63.8)**.
  - Optionally the IMU bosses (M2.5) at (-58.46, 32.56/50.34) and (-38.14, 32.56/50.34).
  - Optionally the Pololu bosses (M2) at (-55.26, -19.84), (-55.26, 1.24) and (-76.34, -19.84).
  - With these, the board can go in without any change to the printed deck.
- **Pi link:** put a 2×5 (or 2×20) header on the board at about **y 34..40, x -27..-12**, parallel to the Pi's GPIO, and connect it with a short IDC ribbon or jumpers.
  - That spot clears the IMU boss at (-38.14, 32.56) and the PB boss at (-30.8, 38.2). No through-hole parts may sit over any boss that is used for mounting. Pins 2/4 carry 5 V, 6 is GND, and 8/10 are UART (GPIO14/15).
- **Servo bus:** place 5 servo-bus connectors at the board edge on the leg axes at r ≈ 72–76:
  - leg 1 (0, 74), leg 2 (-70.4, 22.9), leg 3 (-43.5, -59.9), leg 4 (43.5, -59.9), leg 5 (70.4, 22.9).
  - The cables drop over the deck edge (§5).
  - None of these positions collides with the Pi box or the mounting bosses listed above.
- **IMU:** as close to the centre as the Pi allows, e.g. **(0, -36)** in the south band or (0, 36) in the north band. Align its axes to x/y.
- **Height limits:** top-side parts ≤ 25 mm at the rim and ≤ 35 mm inside r 60. Bottom-side parts ≤ 4 mm.
- **Keep clear:** the Pi cooler intake (keep anything tall away from x -51..14, y -22..22 above z 75) and the posts at r 88.
- **Deck holes:** A (-58, -36) and C (-40, 60) end up under the board and can be ignored or covered. B (0, -71) is the battery-lead entry.

**Alternatives** (for the PCB agent to weigh):
- **(b) Full-disc board r 78 under the Pi.** The board sits on the deck bosses at z 60.5 and the Pi stacks on top with M2.5 standoffs through the board at the Pi hole positions.
  - It gives more area (~19,000 mm²) and lets the Pi plug straight into a board-mounted 2×20 socket.
  - It raises the Pi by ~10–12 mm, to a top of ~90 at r ≤ 59, where the dome is ~100, so it still fits.
- **(c) Board between the top plate and the deck** (z 19.65..52, 32 mm tall, r ≤ ~80 avoiding the lower posts at r 88).
  - It gives the shortest servo leads, since it sits right above the 5 branch slots, and puts the battery right below.
  - It needs a pass-through to the Pi (hole A, B or C), and access is worse. The v5 hub-holder screw holes (3 × Ø 2.5 at (15.6, 9), (-15.6, 9), (0, -18)) and the two LM2596 bosses on the top plate could take it.

## 8. Model structure notes (for importing the PCB STEP later)

- **Script:** `pentapod_build_v6.py` builds the model in stages: `stage_legs_a/b`, `stage_servos`, `stage_instances`, `stage_body('chassis'|'dome'|'misc')`, `stage_hardware` and `stage_pcb`.
- **Root occurrences:** Leg:1..5, Chassis, Dome, Magnets & tie rods, and Bought parts (battery, XT60 lead, loop key, 4 heat-set inserts, Pi 5, Active Cooler).
- **PCB hook:** `stage_pcb` imports `HW_DIR/rocky_control_pcb.step` into a new root occurrence "Rocky control PCB" at `PCB_ORIGIN = (0, 0, DECK_Z1 = 55.5)`, rotated `PCB_ROT` about z.
  - For the ring board on the 5 mm bosses, export the KiCad STEP with its origin at the board centre and the board underside at z 0, then set `PCB_ORIGIN = (0, 0, 60.5)`.
- **Legacy features:** `V5_MOUNTS = True` keeps every v5 hole, slot and boss. Set it to False to drop the legacy deck bosses, holes A, B and C, the hub screws and the LM2596 bosses. The Pi bosses, the power slot and the leg slots always stay.
